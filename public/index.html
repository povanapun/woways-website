Our products

Alongside execution work, Woways builds and runs its own platforms — for students choosing a path, and for companies running their teams.

01
Edtech

Talent Ignition

An edtech platform that closes the gap between what students are taught and what industry expects — turning passive learners into active ones with a clear career path ahead of them.

  • Identifies where a student's academic gap is holding them back
  • Builds knowledge and confidence to close that gap directly
  • Moves students from passive learning to active participation
  • Provides career counselling aligned to real industry expectations
02
Counselling

ConsulBuzz

A platform for students and their parents to make sense of career and college decisions together — built around what the family actually expects from the outcome.

Student Mentor

Counselling for students after 12th, helping them choose the right career path with clarity instead of guesswork.

College Macha

A way to explore colleges, compare them side by side, and land on the one that fits both the student and the parent's expectations.

03
SaaS

Bispun

A CRM built for entities that need to track their day-to-day work clearly — and act on what the data is telling them, with AI helping surface corrections and plans.

  • Tracks regular work across teams in one place
  • Surfaces AI-driven corrections and future plans from the data
  • Works as an entity management portal for the organisation
04
SaaS

Performance CRM

A day-to-day performance tracker for organisations, built so no one misses the appraisal or hike they've earned — and no department loses sight of its own progress.

  • Tracks each employee's KRAs and KPIs continuously, not just at review time
  • Keeps individuals visible for appraisal and hike decisions
  • Rolls up into an entity performance portal across every department
